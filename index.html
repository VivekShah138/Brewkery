What You Are Building

Brewkery is a delightful consumer mobile app where guests browse specialty brews, customize sizes and organic milk choices with real-time price updates, manage their cart, and review checkout details.

🎨 Visual Tone: Warm artisanal aesthetic (Deep Espresso #1a0f0a, Terracotta #d9532f, Amber accents, and Soft Crema #fdf8f4). Clean cards, rounded corners, and crisp product typography.

⚡ How We Evaluate Your AI Engineering Skills

You are expected to use AI coding tools (Claude, Cursor, Copilot, ChatGPT, Gemini). However, copying and pasting unexamined code is an immediate anti-signal. We check:

  • Prompt Efficiency: Providing clean JSON models to the AI without burning context tokens on massive dumps.
  • Architecture Guidance: Directing the AI to maintain strict MVVM (StateFlow, ViewModels, Repository pattern).
  • Critical Bug Detection: Catching floating-point rounding bugs in tax/subtotals or Compose recomposition cycles.

Checklist & Implementation Steps

  • 1
    Project Setup: Native Android project in Kotlin (Android Studio). Compose strongly preferred.
  • 2
    Networking: Retrofit2 or Ktor parsing the provided GitHub data.json endpoint.
  • 3
    State & Price Logic: Dynamic subtotal calculation in ViewModel when item sizes or add-ons change.
  • 4
    Media: Seamless remote image loading with Coil or Glide.
  • 5
    Edge Handling: Proper loading spinner/shimmer and network retry states.
  • 6
    Verification: At least 1 unit test covering cart price calculation or tax math.
