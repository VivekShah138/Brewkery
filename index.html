Build the Brewkery Mobile Experience.

A 4-screen native Android ordering app for an artisanal coffeehouse & bakery. Powered by flexible REST endpoints with dynamic subtotal recalculation and customizable menu options.

What You Are Building

Brewkery is a consumer mobile experience where customers browse artisanal roasts and pastries, customize cup sizes and organic milk choices with instant price calculation, manage their cart, and review checkout details.

🎨 Visual Theme: Warm artisanal coffeehouse palette (Deep Espresso #180d08, Terracotta #d9532f, Amber accents, and Soft Crema #fbf7f4). Clean cards and rounded buttons.

🤖 How We Evaluate Your AI Engineering Skills

Using AI tools (Cursor, Claude, Copilot, ChatGPT, Gemini) is expected. But blind copy-pasting is an anti-signal. We check:

  • Prompt Precision: Providing concise schemas instead of burning context tokens on giant file dumps.
  • Clean Architecture: Enforcing MVVM (StateFlow, ViewModels, Repository pattern).
  • Critical Bug Detection: Catching floating-point tax rounding errors or Compose recomposition cycles.
Mandatory Deliverable: AI_WORKFLOW.md
1. List the AI tools used.
2. Share 2–3 of your best prompts.
3. Explain 1 instance where AI output broke or gave buggy code, and how you diagnosed and fixed it.

Checklist & Implementation Steps

  • 1
    New Project: Initialize in Android Studio with Kotlin (Jetpack Compose strongly preferred).
  • 2
    Networking: Retrofit2 or Ktor parsing either the complete menu or single-item endpoints.
  • 3
    Dynamic Pricing: Subtotal updates in ViewModel as sizes or milk choices change.
  • 4
    Image Loading: Seamless remote image rendering with Coil or Glide.
  • 5
    Robustness: Proper loading spinner and error handling state.
  • 6
    Unit Test: At least 1 unit test covering cart price calculation.
